The Candidate List

Human capacities that have no name yet. Nominated by readers, tested against four thresholds, kept provisional on purpose.

Why a list

Every taxonomy in the history of science began as a list of things somebody noticed before anybody could explain them. Mendeleev had a table with gaps. Linnaeus had drawers of specimens. Before the periodic table of human capacities can exist, somebody has to collect the specimens: the recurring, recognizable human abilities that no personality test measures and no language has bothered to name.

That is what this page is. It is not a scientific instrument. It is the drawer of specimens that precedes one.

The rules

How a nomination becomes an entry

A nomination is a hypothesis. It earns a place only through evidence, and most will not survive. That is not a failure of the list; it is the method working. To graduate from nominated to entry, a capacity has to clear the same four thresholds set out in the founding essay:

  • 01Stable. It shows up in the same person across time and situations, not just once.
  • 02Measurable elsewhere. It can be observed with an instrument other than the anecdote that suggested it.
  • 03Not old wine. It predicts something that intelligence and the Big Five do not already explain.
  • 04Not a Western artifact. It recognizably appears across cultures and languages, which is why nominations from anywhere, in any language, count.

Kairotic sensitivity

Sensing when a system is in phase transition and maximally responsive to intervention. Timing as perception, not luck. Fails in flat stretches; pairs well with grinders.

Semantic load-bearing capacity

Holding an unresolved problem for years without forcing it closed. Not intelligence, not perseverance; a third thing, and possibly the best-kept predictor of fundamental breakthroughs. Closest rival: need for cognitive closure, which measures a comfort rather than a capacity.

Resonance transfer

Inducing an inner state in others independent of what is said. Probably not one capacity but five: calm, urgency, conviction, permission and attention are separate transmitters, and they come apart in the same person.

Retroactive integration

Continually rewriting one's past into a structure that carries the future. The measurable difference between resilience and a past that shifts like loose gravel.

Interstitial perception

Seeing what falls between the categories, the residue every classification leaks. Where most scientific revolutions began.

Separately, a daily sweep of the public record collects strangers describing abilities that have no name. Those are not nominations and they are not entries; they are specimens, credited to the people who wrote them, in The Field.

Nominate

Send the taxonomy an observation

You have almost certainly known someone with an ability that fits no test and no word. The colleague who always knows which week a decision can actually be made. The friend who can carry a contradiction for a decade. The relative who sees what everyone's categories filter out. That observation is data of the informal kind that precedes every formal taxonomy.

A nomination has three parts:
  1. A proposed name, even a clumsy one. Names can be fixed; unnamed things cannot be discussed.
  2. One paragraph describing the capacity: what it does, what it is not (which existing trait it might be confused with), and what its failure mode looks like.
  3. One concrete example from a real life, anonymized. Yours or someone else's.

Nominations in any language are welcome. A capacity has to appear across cultures and languages to become an entry, so a nomination in Japanese, Arabic or Spanish is not a translation problem. It is exactly the data the taxonomy needs.

Nominate by email Or comment on Substack

Every nomination is read. Listed nominations credit the nominator by name or initials, with permission. Nominations are reviewed against the four thresholds; most will remain nominated, some will be discussed in the series, few will become entries. Nothing here measures anyone. See the Charter in the founding essay: the map belongs to the mapped, no selection, every classification expires.
